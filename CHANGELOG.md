# Changelog

## 0.4.0 (test build)

Fixer's texts live on V's phone now, and the terminal looks like the game. Saves from 0.3.0 carry over as they are.

- Every Fixer text is a real conversation on V's phone: Dex's old line, each fixer, and each person asking for work
  get their own thread in the messages list, with their face where the game has one. Answer from the phone: hire or
  pass, ally or take their turf. The terminal's inbox still has everything. (Mod Settings: "Phone threads".)
- New texts pop up the way the game's own do, and the popup's open button takes you to the thread.
- "All of a fixer's gigs" now uses the game's own count for each fixer, the same one the game uses for their district
  reward, so it matches your quest log.
- The terminal (computer site and F7 popup) now uses the game's own colours, frames, button style and sounds, and the
  computer's home page shows it with the game's fixer symbol.
- Fixed: text popups went to a notification queue the game doesn't show texts from, so they could fail to appear.

## 0.3.0 (test build)

The whole city, not just City Center. Test data from 0.2.0 resets on load (your crew and messages stay); the mod
rebuilds everything else from your journal.

- Your fixer career now starts where Dex's ends: after "Playing for Time", his old clients start calling you. Their
  jobs are on your map from day one.
- Every gig you finish can bring someone looking for work.
- Every fixer in the city counts: Regina, Wakako, Dino, Padre, El Capitan, Mr. Hands and Dakota. Finish all of a
  fixer's gigs and they reach out. Ally with them (their jobs on your map, their people, less heat on their streets)
  or take their turf in a war. Win and their district pays you.
- A new FIXERS tab shows every fixer, how far you are through their gigs, and where you stand with them.
- Ally with or beat all seven and you're the King of Night City.
- Fixed: the game's district completion rewards were counted as gigs.

## 0.2.0 (test build)

First test build. Expect rough edges, and keep a backup save.

- Your finished gigs now build your name, including the ones you did before installing. After 5, people start
  texting you for work and you can hire them.
- After 10 gigs, 3 of them in City Center, and Street Cred 30, Dino Dinovic starts watching you. Sooner or later he
  offers you a partnership.
- As Dino's partner you get a fixer terminal: a MAP of City Center with jobs on it. Click a job to see how many people
  it needs, what it pays and your odds with the crew you pick, then send them. They come back hours later with a cut
  for you, or hurt, arrested or dead.
- Your crew level up, get loyal or greedy, and quit or sell you out if you treat them badly. Messy jobs draw NCPD heat.
- Dino gets greedy. Say no and it's war over eight City Center blocks. Hit his turf, guard yours, and survive his
  retaliation.
- Win and City Center is yours: the map opens up to the whole city, including the game's own gigs, NCPD jobs,
  cyberpsychos and car jobs, for your crew to work. Your turf pays you every day.
- The terminal is a website on in-game computers (with Browser Extension Framework), or opens anywhere with F7.
- Every number is adjustable in Mod Settings.
